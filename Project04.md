## Back-Testing Automation: Algorithmic Investment Strategies
Patterns can be identified and predicted in financial markets. Candlestick patterns good indicators that show where price may be high or low. The examples I coded in Pinescript above form part of a set of trading algorithms which place ↑ and ↓ arrows in the market charts. Specifically, this algorithm adds ↓ arrows where it calculates that price is about to fall, in advance of it falling. Likewise, it ads ↑ arrows where it calculates that price will rise, in advance of it rising. Tradingview software is able to subject custom indicators to back-testing that covers the entire history of an assets value. The screenshot above identifies this custom indicator to be accurate for XRP with a 92.59% of having accurately predicted value changes in advance over a period of exactly three years.<br>
